FAQ

Is there a cost to attend?

No. The Agentic Majlis Show is a complimentary, invitation-only event hosted by Ibexa.

Who can attend?

Senior leaders of retail, e-commerce and consumer brands. Seats are limited, and each request is reviewed.

How do I know my seat is confirmed?

Our team will be in touch to confirm your seat, and a calendar invitation follows.

Can I bring a colleague?

Yes, please ask them to request their own seat. Each seat is confirmed individually.

Can I join for part of the day?

Yes, though the best conversations happen over lunch and the reception.

I have dietary needs.

Tell us in the form. Our chefs will take care of you.

Will there be photography?

Yes, a photographer and videographer will capture the day. Let our team know on arrival if you’d prefer not to appear.

Is parking available?

Yes. Parking at Madinat Jumeirah is free for guests.

What if I can no longer attend?

Let us know as early as you can by replying to your confirmation email, so we can offer your seat to someone else.
